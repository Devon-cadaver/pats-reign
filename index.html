HOW TO PLAY

⛵ Sail. Tap BACK, STOP, ROW, HALF or FULL for speed and swipe the wheel to steer (double-tap centres it). Tap the sea or the map to set a course, or ⚓ DOCK to be sailed to the nearest harbour (fleeing any fight; STOP takes the helm back). TOP changes the view; pinch to zoom; ⇄ beside the minimap moves the controls to the other side. Keys: W and S for the sail (tap for one notch, hold to run through), A and D for the wheel, Space throws a spear ahead, C changes the view, E opens the sea chart.

🐍 Monsters. They grow as your ship grows. Name colours show strength: green is weaker, white even, red and purple stronger. Outrun those until you upgrade.

⚔ Fight. Throw spears or fire the ballista at range. Up close a fight starts: hit the weak spot, then dodge the way it shows (keys: Space fires and blocks, A and D dodge left and right). Against ships you form a battle line and they sail past you: fire when a red mark is in the green, and change speed when one aims. Her whole fleet joins the line (the pause menu switches ship fights to CLASSIC).

🎣 Fish. Slow down at bubbling water, cast in the gold band and hold REEL.

⚓ Harbours. Sell fish, repair, buy upgrades and a bigger ship. With Oars & Crew, your crew also mend the hull at sea.

⚡ Weather. In storms, sail into a blue lightning ring for Thor's Fury. Turn your bow into rogue waves. Keep clear of tornadoes and maelstroms (FULL sail breaks free).

🪝 Boarding and treasure. With Grappling Hooks, board a ship under 40% hull to plunder or claim her. A treasure map marks an ✕: sail there, LAND and dig.

☠ Sinking. Half your cargo sinks with the wreck. SALVAGE it at the ☠ or pay a harbour crew. Upgrades are never lost.

💾 Save code. Carry your voyage to another device.

👑 Reign. Slay the Kraken, Jormungandr and the Elder One. Each fights its own way (the Kraken grabs your ship with its tentacles: cut them down and strike its eye; Jormungandr hunts you from below: dodge its red ring, then shoot its head; the Elder One wards itself: repeat its runes to break the ward, and shoot its eye when it flashes if it lifts your ship). In a small ship their young usually rise instead; now and then the legend itself does: flee! Stay in a lair and tougher ones keep rising.
