Pats Reign Bestiary

Every monster and enemy ship in the game as of v0.3.0: 38 monster kinds in 11 families, plus 6 ships. Each one has a picture in the sea (how you meet it) and a full-body picture with the sea taken away, so you can judge the whole model.

Write what you would change in any box and tap Needs a redesign to flag it. There is a box for each kind and one for each whole family (use that for changes to the shared body, like the eel's neck).

Level. The name tag shows Lv. Its colour compares it with your ship: green weaker, white even, orange stronger, red much stronger, purple deadly.
Hull and Hits are its health and one hit before your shields. Monsters come from about one level below your ship to nearly two above, so Meets ships says which ship sizes run into it.
Chasing. A stronger monster chases at 86% of your top speed and gives up after 24 seconds. Touching one starts a battle where it strikes first; tap FIGHT to strike first yourself.

Sharks

5 kinds
  • Fast hunters that cruise just under the surface, so mostly you see the dorsal fin; they leap clear of the water to bite.
  • A shark can take your bait while you fish: a bite starts a battle on the spot.
  • Most common in Home Waters and the Warm Sea.
  • Speed 10.5 (your ships sail at 9 to 13).
  • In battle it lunges from the right, lunges from the left or leaps to bite.

Sea Serpents

7 kinds
  • Long bodies that arch out of the sea in humps behind a rearing neck; the head rises high when it strikes.
  • Every sea has its own breed. Serpent ambushes (the water boils, then one bursts up beside you) use the local breed.
  • Twice as common in the Frost Sea.
  • Speed 9.2 (your ships sail at 9 to 13).
  • In battle it whips its tail from the right, whips its tail from the left or rears up to strike.

Legends

3 bosses
  • They never appear at random. Each sleeps in its lair, marked on the map, and rises when you sail in with a big enough ship.
  • Slay all three for the PAT'S REIGN ending.

The Kraken

Kraken's Maw, far north-east · needs a Snekkja
  • A giant squid head and a ring of long tentacles. Sleeps at its lair until you sail in with a Snekkja or bigger, then rises from the deep at 1.6 times normal size.
  • Speed 6. Attacks three times a turn: sweeps a tentacle from the right, sweeps a tentacle from the left or drags at the hull.

Ships at sea

6 kinds
  • Raider longships come in every ship size (a raider's level is its size). They shoot four-arrow volleys from 12 to 34 away, then fight a ship battle. Half again as common in the Mist Sea.
  • Raider fleets (a sea event) bring two to four ships of one clan, led by a Warlord one size bigger. Sink them all for their war chest.
  • Winning a ship battle pays timber, iron and gold.
