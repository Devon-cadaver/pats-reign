# Valhalla's Tide: the trailer song (for Suno)

Target length: about 60 seconds. Suno tends to run long, so set the clip to **Custom**, paste the lyrics and style below, and if it comes out at 90 seconds or more, trim to the end of the chorus and let the last chord ring, or use "Extend" to tighten the ending.

## Style prompt (paste into the Style box)

Epic Viking folk-metal ballad, 72 bpm, D minor. Opens with a lone clean electric guitar and a deep male voice over wind and waves, then a low war drum and distant horn enter; the chorus explodes into heavy distorted guitars, pounding toms, a male choir and a soaring lead guitar; a short lead guitar solo over the bridge; it ends on one huge sustained power chord and a single horn note. Cinematic, anthemic, sea-shanty swing in the rhythm. Powerful baritone male lead, Norse choir on the chorus. No synths, no modern pop production.

## Lyrics (paste into the Lyrics box)

[Intro – clean guitar, wind and waves, 8 seconds]

[Verse 1 – low voice, one guitar, a slow drum]
Hoist the sail, the sea is black,
The dead are calling, don't look back,
Hold the wheel and hold your breath,
Every wave's a road to death.

[Pre-chorus – drums build, horn]
Serpent rising, kraken's eye,
Steel and fire, do or die!

[Chorus – full band, choir]
VALHALLA'S TIDE! It's calling my name,
Through the monster sea and the burning rain,
VALHALLA'S TIDE! We'll never run,
Row for the gates till the oars are done!

[Bridge – lead guitar solo, 8 bars, choir "oh-oh" under it]

[Outro – all stop, one huge chord, the voice alone]
Row... for Valhalla.

[End – one horn note, waves]

## Notes

- Keep the chorus words exactly as written: "VALHALLA'S TIDE!" is the hook, and it lands twice so the title sticks.
- If Suno makes a second verse, cut it: the commercial needs verse, pre-chorus, chorus, solo, outro and nothing more.
- For the video edit: the clean guitar intro goes under clip 1 (sailing), the pre-chorus under clip 2 (the serpent), the chorus under clips 3 to 5 (the battle line, the harbour, Helheim), the solo over the end card with the gameplay window, and "Row... for Valhalla" over the logo as the last thing heard.
