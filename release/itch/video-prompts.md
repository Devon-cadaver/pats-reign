# Valhalla's Tide: trailer clip prompts

Six clips, each 10 seconds, 9:16 portrait (1080×1920), no music, sound effects only. Photoreal, epic, but every detail matches the game: a Viking longship with a red-and-cream striped square sail, round painted shields along the rails, a carved dragon-head prow, a half-wheel helm at the stern; the sea is deep teal-blue with white foam; the light is low golden sun unless the prompt says otherwise. Keep the camera steady or on one slow move (no spinning, no fast circling).

Paste the shared style block at the end of each prompt.

**Shared style block:** Photorealistic cinematic render, 9:16 vertical, 10 seconds, 24 fps, shallow depth of field, volumetric light, spray and foam detail, no text, no logos, no music, diegetic sound only. Matches the look of the game Valhalla's Tide: Viking longship, red-and-cream striped sail, round shields on the rails, dragon-head prow, teal sea, golden light.

## Clip 1: Sailing (the open sea and the helm)
A Viking longship under full sail cuts through a rolling teal sea at golden hour, seen from just behind and above the stern. At the stern a bearded Viking in a red tunic hauls a great wooden half-wheel; the ship heels and the striped sail fills with a boom of canvas. Green pine islands and a small harbour with a wooden dock slide past on the left; snow peaks glow far off on the horizon. A pod of porpoises leaps beside the bow. The camera eases slowly forward over the deck as the bow rises and falls, spray flying over the shields. Sound: wind in the sail, creaking timbers, oars dipping, gulls, waves breaking on the hull.

## Clip 2: The sea serpent (a monster fight)
Low camera at the waterline beside the longship. The sea bulges, then an enormous emerald sea serpent with a gold diamond pattern and glowing eyes rears up out of the water beside the ship, humps of its body arching behind it, water pouring off its scales. The Viking crew brace behind their shields; a heavy ballista on the deck fires a glowing bolt that strikes the serpent's neck with a burst of spray; the serpent whips its tail at the hull and shields splinter. One slow push-in on the serpent's head as it screams. Sound: the roar of the serpent, the ballista's thunk and the bolt whistling, splintering wood, crashing water, the crew shouting.

## Clip 3: The ship battle (the battle line)
Two Viking longships sail side by side on parallel courses at sunset, forty metres apart, in a ship-to-ship battle. From the deck of ours, behind the ballista, we watch the enemy ship with a black sail and a red emblem cross our line of fire; our ballista fires a volley of three bolts that arc across the water and smash into her hull and rigging, her sail tears, fire catches on her deck and black smoke rolls across the sea. Her crew loose a volley of burning arrows back at us; arrows thud into our shields. Slow steady camera, the two ships rising and falling together. Sound: ballista fire, bolts whistling, wood cracking, arrows hissing and thudding, fire crackling, war horns.

## Clip 4: The harbour and the shipwright (upgrading)
Dawn in a Viking harbour of timber halls on a green island. A longship rests at a wooden dock while shipwrights hammer iron plates onto her hull and hoist a new sail: a deep blue sail with white Valkyrie wings. Gold coins pour from a chest onto a trestle table; a shipwright unrolls a parchment showing a far bigger ship. Pull back along the dock to reveal the ship now bristling with new shields, twin ballistas and glowing rune-carved iron, ready to sail. Sound: hammers on iron, saws, rope creaking, coins clinking, gulls, a harbour bell, water lapping at the dock.

## Clip 5: Helheim (the underworld and the legends)
A great black longship with a skull-and-horns figurehead passes through a ring of standing rune stones on the water into another world: the sky turns to a burning red and orange, ash falls like snow, and the sea goes dark and still. Glowing purple soul-lights drift over the water. Ahead a vast undead kraken, bone white with glowing green eyes, rises from the black sea, tentacles thicker than the ship lifting on every side, and a draugr fleet with ragged glowing sails sails out of the murk behind it. Slow push forward through the gate. Sound: a deep horn, the hiss of ash, groaning stone, the kraken's rumble, water cascading off its tentacles, distant wailing.

## Clip 6: The end card (title above, gameplay window below)

Middle and bottom half of the frame: thick glowing red-orange fog rolling slowly, like the burning sky of Helheim, lit from within, with embers drifting upward. Keep the centre of the fog area clear and even so a rectangle of real gameplay footage (roughly 60% of the frame width, 9:16, centred, from about 30% to 85% of the frame height) can be laid over it. No text other than the logo. The logo holds still; only the fog and embers move. Sound: a low wind, embers crackling, a single deep war horn at the end.
